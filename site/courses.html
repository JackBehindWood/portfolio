Courses

Most relevant to optimisation, risk and quantitative work

AE4321-15 Air Traffic Management

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

An introduction to air traffic management and its research topics. It covers airspace and flight planning, communication, navigation and surveillance, separation and conflict geometry. It also covers newer concepts such as trajectory-based operations.

AE4353 Artificial Intelligence for Aerospace Control and Operations

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

Machine learning for aerospace problems, with the emphasis on deep neural networks. It covers both the theory of how these methods learn and their application to regression and classification tasks.

AE4422-20 Agent-based Modelling and Simulation in Air Transport

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

Agent-based modelling and simulation applied to air transport. Topics include multi-agent systems, emergence, coordination and planning, reinforcement learning, auctions and game theory. It also covers how to analyse and validate such models.

AE4423-20 Airline Planning and Optimisation

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

Planning problems of airlines at strategic, tactical and operational level, such as fleet planning, network development, scheduling and maintenance planning. The course focuses on building models for these problems and linking them to operational consequences.

AE4441-16 Operations Optimisation

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

Modelling and solving optimisation problems, with examples from air transport. It covers linear programming and the simplex method, duality and sensitivity analysis, network problems, dynamic programming, mixed integer programming and nonlinear programming.

AE4446 Airport and Cargo Operations

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

Airport design and operations on the landside and airside, as well as the air cargo supply chain. Several lectures treat the related optimisation problems, including gate assignment, taxiing, bin packing, scheduling and an introduction to stochastic programming.

AE4465 Maintenance Modelling and Analysis

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

Maintenance in aviation seen through reliability, probability and statistics, with a look at newer data-driven methods. Students fit lifetime distributions and use them to optimise preventive maintenance strategies.

EPA143A Macro-economics for Policy Analysis

MSc TU Delft · 5 EC

An introduction to macroeconomics for policy analysis. It explains how a market economy works and where it falls short and how money and the financial sector function. It also looks at how fiscal and monetary policy affect income, employment and competitiveness.

WI4062TU Transport, Routing and Scheduling

MSc TU Delft · 3 EC

Combinatorial optimisation for transport and production problems. Topics include shortest paths, assignment and transportation problems, the travelling salesman problem, vehicle routing and job shop scheduling, solved with exact and heuristic methods.

WI4771TU Object Oriented Scientific Programming with C++

MSc TU Delft · 3 EC

Scientific programming in modern C++ with an object-oriented approach. It also covers debugging code written by a person or an AI agent and refactoring it for readability.

Other courses

AE1205 Programming and Scientific Computing in Python for AE1

BSc TU Delft · 2 EC

A first programming course in Python for aerospace students. It moves from general-purpose programming to scientific computing with NumPy and Matplotlib, then to numerical simulation and animation with PyGame.

AE1240 Physics

BSc TU Delft · 6 EC

Classical physics for engineers, covering oscillations and waves, sound, electromagnetism and optics. Students practise recognising these phenomena in aerospace settings and turning them into mathematical models.

AE3200 Design Synthesis

BSc TU Delft · 15 EC

The capstone design project of the BSc, in which a group takes an aircraft, spacecraft or wind turbine design from requirements through concept selection to a final design. It trains structured, iterative design work and the handling of conflicting demands.

AE4010 Research Methodologies

MSc TU Delft · 2 EC

Preparation for the MSc thesis. It covers framing a research question, choosing methods, quantitative and qualitative data analysis, validation, citing literature and writing a research plan.

AE4302 Avionics and Operations

MSc TU Delft · 3 EC

Aircraft navigation and flight-deck systems, from air data, gyroscopes and compasses to radio and satellite navigation. It also looks at how these systems fit into the wider air navigation and air traffic management picture.

AE4314-21 Helicopter Performance, Stability and Control

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

Helicopter and rotorcraft fundamentals: configurations, performance in hover, climb and forward flight, blade dynamics, control and handling. It also introduces aeroelasticity.

AE4431-23 Aircraft Noise

MSc TU Delft · 3 EC

The physics of sound and how it applies to aircraft. It runs from wave propagation and the decibel scale through noise generation and propagation to assessing and reducing noise around airports.

AE4462-17 Aircraft Emissions and Climate Effects

MSc TU Delft · 4 EC

How aviation affects climate and air quality through CO2 and non-CO2 effects such as NOx and contrails. It combines basic atmospheric science with the case for operational and technological mitigation.

AE5026 Master Thesis Aerospace Engineering

MSc TU Delft · 45 EC

The 45 EC graduation project of the MSc. Students plan and carry out an independent research or design project on a focused question and report on it at an academic level.

AE5051 Internship

MSc TU Delft · 15 EC

A full-time internship of at least ten weeks outside TU Delft, at a company, research institute or another university. Students apply their engineering knowledge in a professional setting and report on it.

WI1402LR Calculus II

BSc TU Delft · 5 EC

Multivariable calculus: partial and directional derivatives and extreme values, then double and triple integrals in various coordinate systems. It also covers vector fields and line and surface integrals.

WI1403LR Linear Algebra

BSc TU Delft · 5 EC

Vectors and matrices and what they are used for: solving linear systems, linear transformations, projections, least squares and systems of linear differential equations.

WI1421LR Calculus I

BSc TU Delft · 6 EC

Single-variable calculus and its foundations for engineering: vectors, differentiation, integration techniques, first-order differential equations and complex numbers.

WM0324LR Ethics and Engineering for Aerospace Engineering

MSc TU Delft · 3 EC

The responsibility of engineers, seen through philosophy, history and law. Topics include codes of ethics and reasoning about risk and uncertainty. Students also look at responsibility within organisations and apply the theory to an engineering case.
