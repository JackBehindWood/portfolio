TraffixGen traffic generator for BlueSky

team project · MSc, Control and Operations Project (AE4270), report November 2025, team of two

TraffixGen is a modular Python library that generates realistic air traffic scenarios from historical EUROCONTROL flight data, so that air traffic management simulations such as BlueSky can use traffic that follows real spatial and temporal patterns. It was built in a team of two. My part was the software architecture and three modules: Dataset, Distribution and Flight State Space.

Method

  • Architecture: three modules with clear interfaces, so each can be reused or replaced without changing the rest.
  • Dataset: loads flight data from CSV, JSON and Parquet into pandas, filters it by area and altitude with Delaunay triangulation, derives motion features such as ground speed, vertical speed, heading and climb angle. It also groups the data by flight.
  • Distribution: one interface for continuous and discrete distributions built on scipy.stats. Fits are ranked by log-likelihood, AIC or BIC for model selection.
  • Flight State Space: samples origin-destination pairs and aircraft types from the fitted distributions, then generates trajectories with kernel density estimation or tree-based regression. A phase-specific variant models take-off, cruise and landing separately.

Limits

The kernel density estimation models and the polygon filtering with boundary interpolation are experimental and need further validation. No accuracy results are reported.

Key facts

  • Jack was responsible for the TraffixGen software architecture and for the Dataset, Distribution and Flight State Space modules
  • The kernel density estimation state spaces and the polygon filtering with boundary interpolation are experimental and need further validation; the report gives no accuracy figures
