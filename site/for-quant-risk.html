For quantitative and risk roles

The work most relevant to quantitative research, trading and risk: simulation, statistical testing and models checked until they fail.

  • Thesis: evaluated across 600,000 simulated aircraft. Models and policies were selected with statistical tests (Diebold-Mariano, Wilcoxon signed-rank, Mann-Whitney, with a Holm-Bonferroni correction), and key metrics were computed by two independent paths.
  • A failure I traced: adding heading information collapsed success on closely spaced parallel runways to 77%. The cause was the approach direction, and the final model leaves that information out with no loss in performance.
  • Orakle: a Monte Carlo simulation of a whole tournament with an ensemble of Dixon-Coles, Poisson regression and XGBoost models, ranking every prediction by expected points.
